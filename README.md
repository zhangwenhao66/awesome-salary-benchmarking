# Awesome-Salary-Benchmarking

# Top Salary Benchmarking Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Compensation Benchmarking, Pay Equity, Market Pricing, Salary Bands & Total Rewards Data*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Salary Benchmarking**. These tools provide market compensation data, real-time or survey-based benchmarks, pay band design, equity analysis, job leveling, and total rewards insights to help organizations set competitive and equitable pay.

**Examples** include Pave, Ravio, Payscale, Salary.com, Figures, CompAnalyst, OpenComp, ERI, Comptryx, and Radford McLagan (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for internal pay transparency, open compensation data collection, public salary datasets, scrapers, and self-hosted benchmarking tools — ideal for HR teams, compensation analysts, startups, and developers seeking greater transparency and control over pay data.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Pave](https://www.pave.com/)**  
  Modern compensation platform offering real-time benchmarking via HRIS integrations, pay planning, offer tools, and market data for tech and growth-stage companies.

- **[Ravio](https://ravio.com/)**  
  Real-time European and global tech compensation benchmarking with employer-sourced data, pay equity features, and salary band management.

- **[Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/)**  
  Established compensation data and software provider with extensive survey-based market pricing, job matching, and analytics tools.

- **[Salary.com](https://www.salary.com/)**  
  Comprehensive compensation platform featuring CompAnalyst for market data, salary structures, job pricing, and AI-assisted workflows.

- **[Figures](https://www.figures.hr/)**  
  European-focused compensation management and benchmarking solution with strong support for pay transparency and compliance needs.

- **[CompAnalyst (Salary.com)](https://www.salary.com/)**  
  Mature market-pricing and benchmarking tool within Salary.com for structured salary bands, analytics, and enterprise compensation management.

- **[OpenComp](https://www.opencomp.com/)**  
  Compensation decision software with real-time crowd-sourced/HRIS data, benchmarking, merit cycles, and pay strategy tools.

- **[ERI (Economic Research Institute)](https://www.erieri.com/)**  
  Long-standing provider of salary survey data, geographic differentials, and compensation analytics for a wide range of roles and locations.

- **[Comptryx](https://www.comptryx.com/)**  
  Compensation benchmarking and analytics platform supporting market pricing and total rewards insights.

- **[Radford McLagan (Aon)](https://radford.aon.com/)**  
  Premier compensation database and survey platform especially strong in technology, life sciences, and financial services roles.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[CeleryBand](https://github.com/maybeanerd/celeryband)**  
  Open-source platform for anonymous internal salary sharing and comparison within an organization to promote pay transparency and fair compensation awareness.

- **[Comp Pulse](https://github.com/jschulman/comp-pulse)**  
  Open-source dashboard tracking directional compensation trends (index-based, not absolute salaries) from public job-board data in specific markets.

- **[levels.fyi related scrapers & tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Community scrapers, APIs, and analysis scripts that extract and analyze publicly available tech compensation data from levels.fyi and similar sources.

- **[open-salaries](https://github.com/sizovs/open-salaries)**  
  Curated collection of companies, case studies, and resources promoting transparent and open salary policies.

- **[Compflow](https://github.com/Dogukkanx/compflow)**  
  Open-source style tools focused on salary bands, approval workflows, and pay equity insights (particularly relevant for EU Pay Transparency Directive contexts).

- **[Academic & ML salary benchmarking frameworks](https://github.com/ApexPredictors/COMP6940-Project)**  
  Interpretable machine-learning projects for cross-country salary prediction, wage anomaly detection, and fairness analysis using open datasets.

- **[LeetCode / regional compensation aggregators](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source projects that collect and analyze publicly shared compensation data for specific regions or tech communities (e.g., India-focused insights).

- **[BLS / government open data pipelines](https://www.bls.gov/)**  
  Tools and scripts for working with free public sources such as U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS).

- **[Internal pay transparency calculators](https://github.com/)**  
  Community implementations of formula-based or band-based open salary systems inspired by companies practicing radical transparency.

- **[Job-board salary disclosure monitors](https://github.com/)**  
  Scripts that track the percentage of job postings disclosing salary ranges and extract available compensation signals from public APIs.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Public datasets from levels.fyi community exports, Glassdoor aggregates (where legally scrapable), and government statistical agencies.
- Spreadsheet templates and simple web apps for building internal salary bands.
- Pay equity analysis notebooks using open HR datasets and fairness metrics.
- Integration examples with HRIS open APIs for internal benchmarking dashboards.
- Resources and calculators inspired by GitLab’s open compensation philosophy and similar transparent companies.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine public data sources (BLS, levels.fyi community data, job-board disclosures) with internal HRIS extracts, store in a privacy-preserving database, apply leveling frameworks, visualize bands with open BI tools (Metabase, Grafana, or custom React), and use local LLMs for natural-language market summaries and equity gap explanations. Deploy internal tools like **CeleryBand** for anonymous peer comparisons.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Compensation data quality varies significantly by source (employer-reported vs. self-reported vs. public job postings). Always validate methodology, sample size, and recency before making pay decisions.
- Self-hosted and open-source solutions handling employee salary data must comply with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) and internal confidentiality policies.
